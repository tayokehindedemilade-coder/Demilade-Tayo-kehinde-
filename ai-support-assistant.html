PROJECT 03 / AI AUTOMATION

AI Support Assistant.

An AI-powered customer support concept designed to answer common questions, assist customers and help businesses manage enquiries more efficiently.

← Back to Portfolio
01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Customer support teams often receive repeated questions about products, services, payments and general enquiries. This project explores how an AI assistant can provide useful responses to common questions while allowing complex enquiries to be escalated to a human support representative.

02 / WORKFLOW

How It Works

STEP 01

Customer Message

A customer submits a question or enquiry through a communication channel.

STEP 02

AI Processing

The AI analyses the message and identifies what the customer needs.

STEP 03

Response Generation

The assistant prepares a relevant response using available information.

STEP 04

Human Escalation

Complex or unresolved enquiries are forwarded to a human support agent.

03 / FEATURES

Key Features

  • Automated responses to common questions.
  • Understanding customer enquiries using AI.
  • Consistent and organised communication.
  • Escalation of complex issues to human agents.
  • Reduced repetitive work for support teams.
04 / TECHNOLOGY

Tools & Technologies

  • OpenAI – AI-powered response generation.
  • n8n – Workflow automation and integration.
  • Webhooks – Receiving incoming messages.
  • APIs – Connecting different services.
05 / OBJECTIVE

Project Goal

The objective is to explore how AI and automation can improve customer support, reduce response delays and allow human agents to focus on more complicated customer issues.
