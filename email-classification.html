PROJECT 02 / AI AUTOMATION

AI Email Classification.

An intelligent email automation concept designed to analyse incoming messages, identify their purpose and organise them into the appropriate business departments.

← Back to Portfolio
01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Businesses receive emails from customers, employees and other organisations every day. Sorting these messages manually can consume time and delay responses. This project explores how AI can automatically analyse incoming emails and classify them according to their content, making email management more organised and efficient.

02 / WORKFLOW

How It Works

The proposed workflow follows a simple sequence to process and organise incoming messages.

STEP 01

Email Trigger

Detects a new incoming email through an email trigger.

STEP 02

AI Analysis

AI examines the subject and message content to understand the purpose of the email.

STEP 03

Classification

The message is assigned to the most appropriate department.

STEP 04

Routing

The classified email is directed to the appropriate department or workflow.

03 / CATEGORIES

Email Classification Categories

  • Sales – Product enquiries and sales requests.
  • Customer Service – Complaints and customer support.
  • HR – Recruitment and employee-related messages.
  • Finance – Payments, invoices and financial enquiries.
  • Operations – Internal and operational matters.
  • Other – Messages that do not fit the listed categories.
04 / TECHNOLOGY

Tools & Technologies

  • n8n – Workflow automation.
  • OpenAI – AI-powered email analysis.
  • IMAP Email Trigger – Detecting incoming messages.
  • Conditional Routing – Directing classified emails.
05 / OBJECTIVE

Project Goal

The goal is to explore how artificial intelligence and workflow automation can reduce repetitive administrative tasks, improve email organisation and help businesses manage incoming communication more efficiently.
