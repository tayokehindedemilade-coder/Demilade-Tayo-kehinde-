PROJECT 04 / AUTOMATION

Lead Management

A lead management workflow designed to collect, organise and track potential customers while making follow-up processes more efficient.

Project Overview

This project explores how automation can help businesses manage incoming leads, store customer information and keep track of potential sales opportunities.

How the Workflow Works

  • Lead information is collected through a form.
  • Customer details are sent to a central database.
  • Leads are organised based on their information.
  • Follow-up tasks are created for potential customers.
  • Lead status is updated as the sales process progresses.

Key Features

  • Automated lead collection
  • Customer information management
  • Lead status tracking
  • Follow-up organisation
  • Reduced manual data entry

Tools & Technologies

n8n Google Sheets Webhooks APIs Automation

Project Goal

To develop a practical understanding of how automation can improve customer relationship management and help businesses handle leads more effectively.
